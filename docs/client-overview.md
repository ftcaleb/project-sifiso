# Sifiso Holdings — website demo

**Live demo:** https://sifiso-holdings-enh.pages.dev

## What this is

A working demonstration of a new website for Sifiso Holdings. It presents the business as the operating system for Africa's built environment: infrastructure asset management, spatial planning and GIS, valuations, and the MERIDIAN™ platform. Every section moves, the figures tick live, and the design is built to stand beside the best firms in the industry.

## Pages to look at

| Page | What to look at |
|---|---|
| [Home](https://sifiso-holdings-enh.pages.dev/) | The first impression: a 3D city model that follows your mouse, the four services, live figures and a MERIDIAN™ preview. |
| [MERIDIAN™](https://sifiso-holdings-enh.pages.dev/meridian) | The flagship product page. Click the four modules and watch the dashboards change. |
| [Services](https://sifiso-holdings-enh.pages.dev/services) | The four service areas, one after another, with lines that draw as you scroll. |
| [About](https://sifiso-holdings-enh.pages.dev/about) | Story, values, timeline and leadership. |
| [Sectors](https://sifiso-holdings-enh.pages.dev/sectors) | Who the firm serves, and a track-record table. |
| [Insights](https://sifiso-holdings-enh.pages.dev/insights) | Articles. Open one to see the article layout. |
| [Team](https://sifiso-holdings-enh.pages.dev/team) | The people. Open a name to see the profile page. |
| [Contact](https://sifiso-holdings-enh.pages.dev/contact) | A working enquiry form (it emails you), an office map, and a QR code maker you can use yourself. |

Tip: the **Index** button, top right on every page, opens the full site map.

## This is a demo

All names, figures, projects, articles and photographs are placeholders chosen to show the design. Nothing is final. Wording, colours, images, pages, the order of sections, the people and the numbers can all be changed to suit how you want Sifiso Holdings presented.
